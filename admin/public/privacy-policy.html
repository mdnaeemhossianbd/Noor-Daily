Privacy Policy for Noor Daily

Last updated: October 2026

Welcome to Noor Daily. Your privacy and trust are of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and how your data is protected.

1. Information We Collect

  • Location Data: If you grant location permission, Noor Daily uses your device's geographical coordinates solely on-device to calculate astronomical prayer times (Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha). Your location coordinates are never saved to our servers, sold, or shared with third parties.
  • Bookmarked Articles & Settings: Your saved articles, language preferences, and calculation settings are stored strictly locally on your device via standard local storage.
  • Advertising & Analytics: We use Google AdMob to serve non-intrusive advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and use pseudonymous device identifiers and diagnostic data for ad performance in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide accurate solar prayer schedules and Qibla alignments.
  • To send timely local prayer reminder notifications on your device.
  • To display dynamic Quranic verses, Hadith collections, and Islamic articles.

3. Cloud Services & Data Security

Our dynamic content is powered by Google Firebase. Public users can access published Islamic articles without creating an account or logging in. Administrative actions are restricted exclusively to authorized administrators via Firebase Authentication.

4. Children's Privacy

Noor Daily does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@noordaily.com
